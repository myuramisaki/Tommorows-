PLAY THE CURSE RULES

TOMORROW HORROR

You wake up in a room you do not recognize. The date on the wall says October 17. But you remember going to sleep on October 15. There is no window. The door is locked. And every time you close your eyes you wake up in the same place one day further from home.

Something is feeding on your tomorrows. It does not want you to leave. It wants to watch you run out of days. Three rooms. Three puzzles. One chance to break the loop before you vanish completely.

Each wrong choice brings you closer to the final yesterday.
The truth hides in what repeats.
Tomorrow is running out.

THE CURSE OF THE ETERNAL YESTERDAY

Others have been here. You found their marks scratched into the walls. They all wrote the same thing. It does not kill you. It just removes your future. One day at a time. Until there is nothing left but the memory of what you used to be.

The thing that lives here feeds on potential. Every tomorrow you will never see becomes its meal. To escape you must give it something else to eat. Or you must starve it. Or you must stay here forever and let it feed only on you.

There is no happy ending. There is only the ending you can live with.

RULES OF SURVIVAL

Read every note. The answers are never hidden. They are just written in places most people do not bother to look.

Time loops. What happened once will happen again. The pattern is the key. The numbers repeat. Trust the pattern.

Choose carefully. Some doors lead deeper. Some lead nowhere. One leads out. And one leads to the end of everything. You will not know until you turn the handle.

Do not stay in the same room too long. The silence grows teeth.

ROOM 1 THE WAITING CHAMBER

ENDING I THE SACRIFICE

You seal the loop from the inside. The door closes. The others are free. The world outside continues. You remain here. Every day is the same. Every tomorrow is the same as today. It will never hunger again. It will never let you go. You saved them all. And you will never see the sun again.

ENDING II THE BROKEN CYCLE

You destroy the mechanism. The curse unravels. You wake up in your bed. October 15. It was all a dream. But the marks on your hands are still there. And sometimes when you look at the sunset it stares back. You are free. But something followed you home. And it knows where you sleep.

ENDING III THE NEW GUARD

You take its place. The hunger transfers. The doors open. You walk out. But you are not the same. Every night you feel the pull. Every tomorrow tastes like possibility. You can leave. But you know you will return. Someone has to hold the door. And now it is your turn.
